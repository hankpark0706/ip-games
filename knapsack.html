Pack the Rucksack

Knapsack Problem

Your rucksack has a weight capacity. Each piece of gear has a weight (kg) and a mission value (pts). Choose which items to pack — without exceeding the capacity — to maximize total value. Click gear to pack or unpack, then Submit. Everyone works the same loadout — hit Reset to clear your picks and start over.
