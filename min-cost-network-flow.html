Minimum Cost Network Flow

Two supply depots must resupply two divisions over a road network, either directly or through a forward relay hub (which only passes convoys through). Each road has a cost per truckload and a capacity. Set the flow on every road — right on the arc — so each node's flow balances, at the lowest total cost. Submit when every node is balanced.
