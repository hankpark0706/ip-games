Markowitz Portfolio

You have ₩1,000,000 to invest for one year across three assets, in ₩100,000 lots. Drag a bar up or down (or tap −/+) to build your portfolio. You may leave cash uninvested (it earns nothing), but never spend more than the budget. Reach an expected profit of at least ₩100,000 (a 10% return on the fund) with the smallest possible risk, the standard deviation of your profit. Everyone faces the same market — hit Reset to start over.
