Transportation Problem

Two supply depots ship to three front-line divisions. Every depot can reach every division, but there is no relay hub — that bipartite shape is exactly what makes this a transportation problem (min-cost flow with sources on one side, sinks on the other). Set the flow on each road so every depot empties (∑ out = supply) and every division is supplied (∑ in = demand), at the lowest total cost.
